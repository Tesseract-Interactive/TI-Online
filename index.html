How an engagement runs

Four stages, start to delivery.

01

Discover

Brief, references, and technical constraints gathered — file formats, poly budgets, deadlines, deliverable specs.

02

Block

Greybox model, camera, and composition locked before any surfacing work begins. Cheap to change here.

03

Render

Materials, lighting, and final passes. Iterative reviews against the locked composition, not from scratch.

04

Deliver

Full-resolution renders, engine-ready assets, and a defined round of revisions — handed off in the formats you need.
